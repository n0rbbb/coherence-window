What is this?
Shows when the Moon is in your zodiac sign in 2026 — in both the tropical (season-anchored) and sidereal (star-anchored) systems.

The union window spans from when the tropical window opens to when the sidereal window closes.

Use the day/month steppers to move through 2026 and see the Moon's position and window status for any date.

Technical notes
Tropical windows derived by applying a δ ≈ 1.836 day offset (ayanamsa 24.18° ÷ lunar speed 13.17°/day). The union window spans from tropical entry to sidereal exit — the widest conservative estimate.

Moon position is approximated from a linear model anchored to the Jan 27 2026 Gemini entry. Accurate to within a few hours; not ephemeris-grade.
